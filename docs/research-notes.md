# Research notes (external sources)

## Mario Kart 8 Deluxe drifting — https://vikemk.com/drifting-guide
- Drift charge counter increases each frame; spark colour shows tier: blue = Mini-Turbo, orange = Super MT, purple = Ultra MT. Charging is faster when the stick is held >45° into the turn (5/frame vs 2/frame).
- Thresholds ~250/530/820 counter units → at optimal charge roughly 0.9 s / 1.8 s / 2.8 s. Higher tier = longer boost on release.
- Delay drift: wait until wheels land after hop before steering; soft drifting (not full lock) lets you charge optimally without running wide. Every turn costs speed; neutral hops preserve it. Counter-hop widens the start/exit angle to charge more.
- Takeaway: visible, colour-coded charge tiers + a readable "release now" moment = the core skill loop.

## Forza Horizon skill chains — https://forza.fandom.com/wiki/Forza_Horizon_4/Skills ; Steam FH6 thread
- Skills (drift, air, near miss, drafting, wreckage) within a couple of seconds of each other chain; multiplier rises to x5; chain banks after a timeout, lost on crash.
- Takeaway: a style-combo meter rewards continuous expressive driving and gives constant HUD feedback.

## Burnout / sense of speed — reddit r/Burnout, Liam Svirk blog (motion blur for speed)
- Motion blur, FOV widening, camera shake and speed lines amplify perceived speed; crashes in slow motion.

## Game juice — https://www.gameanalytics.com/blog/squeezing-more-juice-out-of-your-game-design
- Juice must echo core gameplay: screen shake, squash/stretch, particles, sound layering, hit-stop.
